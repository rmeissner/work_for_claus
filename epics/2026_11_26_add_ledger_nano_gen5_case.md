## Overview

## Architecture Decision

The case is one closed sleeve (a rectangular tube with a floor), generated by
`models/ledger-nano-gen5-case/generate.py` like the other generated models. It uses only existing
toolkit functions (`round_corners`, `extrude`, `assert_printable`, `write_stl`). It adds no new
toolkit helpers, dependencies or webapp changes, apart from the catalog test expectation.

It prints standing upright on its closed end with the opening facing up. Every wall is then
vertical, so it needs no supports, and the opening needs no bridging. A thumb notch on both large
faces at the open end lets the user push the device out.

### Alternatives Considered

- **Two-part box with lid:** better protection, but two parts, a fit to tune between them and twice
  the review. Rejected because it isn't the simplest option.
- **Open bumper with lips over the front:** keeps the touchscreen and USB-C usable in the case, but
  the lips are overhangs that need supports or tight tolerances. Rejected for simplicity.
- **Flat-printed sleeve (lying on a large face):** the open end and notches would need bridging over
  ~55 mm. Printing upright avoids that.

## Tech Specs

- Device size (from [Ledger's announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251023549325/en)):
  **79.40 × 53.35 × 8.64 mm**. Corner radius isn't published: use a named parameter, default 5 mm.
- Named parameters in `generate.py`: device size, `CLEARANCE` (default 0.3 mm per side), `WALL`
  (default 1.6 mm, 4 perimeters with a 0.4 mm nozzle), `FLOOR` (default 1.6 mm), corner radius,
  thumb notch width/depth.
- Cavity = device + 2 × clearance; outside = cavity + 2 × wall, corners rounded by radius + wall.
  The device sits a few mm proud of the opening, so the cavity height is the device length minus a
  grip margin (parameter, default 10 mm). The thumb notches take away more wall so it can be pushed out.
- Output: one STL, `ledger-nano-gen5-case.stl` (about 57 × 13 × 72 mm), listed in `project.json`
  with version `1.0` and a description that gives the print settings (PLA, upright, no supports)
  and says the fit hasn't been tested on a real device yet.
- Validation: `generate.py` asserts that the sleeve is one connected, printable solid
  (`assert_printable`, `decompose()`). `pytest` checks that the committed STL matches the script.
  Add the slug to the expected list in `webapp/src/models/buildModels.test.ts`. Check the preview
  in the webapp and attach a screenshot to the PR.

## Open Questions and Assumptions

- The sleeve covers the touchscreen and USB-C: it's for carrying and storage, and the device is
  taken out to use. Confirm that's fine, or ask for an open-front bumper instead.
- The corner radius (5 mm) and clearance (0.3 mm) are estimates; they may need to be tuned after
  a test print on Claus.
- PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers. No magnets, lanyard hole or engraving.
